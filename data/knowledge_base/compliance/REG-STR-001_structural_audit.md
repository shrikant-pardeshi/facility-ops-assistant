# Structural Audit and Structural Stability Certificate

> **Illustrative paraphrase for demonstration only - not legal text.** Requirements, intervals and authorities vary by jurisdiction and are amended from time to time. Always verify against the current Act, Rules and the local authority before acting.

## Requirement
Local bodies in Maharashtra commonly require periodic structural audits for older buildings (for example, buildings more than 30 years old), carried out by a registered/empanelled structural engineer. A structural stability certificate is also often requested by the fire authority at the time of Fire NOC renewal, regardless of building age.

## Scope of a structural audit
- Visual survey of columns, beams, slabs, and external walls for cracks, spalling, exposed or corroded reinforcement, and deflection.
- Terrace, podium and basement checks for water ingress and waterproofing failure.
- Non-destructive tests where required (rebound hammer, ultrasonic pulse velocity, cover meter, carbonation / half-cell potential).
- Review of any unauthorised structural changes (openings in slabs or walls, added loads).

## FM team responsibilities
1. Maintain the certificate validity; internal policy is to commission the next audit at least 90 days before expiry.
2. Report and track persistent seepage or cracking near structural members between audits (see SOP-PLMB-001).
3. Implement repairs recommended by the structural engineer and keep completion records.
