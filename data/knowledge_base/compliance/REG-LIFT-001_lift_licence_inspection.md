# Lift Licence and Periodic Inspection

> **Illustrative paraphrase for demonstration only - not legal text.** Requirements, intervals and authorities vary by jurisdiction and are amended from time to time. Always verify against the current Act, Rules and the local authority before acting.

## Requirement
Every lift must hold a valid licence to operate issued by the state Inspector of Lifts. The lift must be periodically inspected by the Inspector (commonly annually), and the licence renewed, for the lift to continue in lawful operation. The licence (or its particulars) should be displayed in or near the lift car.

## FM team responsibilities
1. Track each lift's licence expiry date separately - licences are per lift, not per building.
2. Apply for inspection / renewal at least 45 days before expiry (internal policy).
3. Before the inspection, have the AMC vendor complete a full safety check: brakes, overspeed governor and safety gear, door locks, emergency alarm and intercom, ARD (automatic rescue device), and pit/machine room condition.
4. Keep the lift maintenance register (monthly vendor visits) up to date; the Inspector may ask for it.

## Accident reporting
Any accident involving a lift that causes injury must be reported to the Inspector of Lifts without delay, and the lift kept out of service until the Inspector permits its use.

## Consequences of lapse
A lift operating without a valid licence is a statutory non-compliance. Internal policy: a lift whose licence has expired should be taken out of service if the Inspector or the owner so directs, and the lapse escalated to the Facility Manager.
