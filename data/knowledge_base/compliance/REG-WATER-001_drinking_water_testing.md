# Drinking Water Quality Testing

> **Illustrative paraphrase for demonstration only - not legal text.** Requirements, intervals and authorities vary by jurisdiction and are amended from time to time. Always verify against the current Act, Rules and the local authority before acting.

## Requirement (internal policy)
Drinking water supplied to occupants must meet the acceptable limits of IS 10500 (Drinking Water - Specification). The FM team tests drinking water quarterly through an NABL-accredited laboratory, with samples from the underground tank, overhead tank, and at least two dispenser points.

## Parameters
Physical and chemical (pH, turbidity, TDS, hardness, chlorides, iron, residual chlorine) and bacteriological (total coliform, E. coli).

## FM team responsibilities
1. Clean and disinfect water tanks half-yearly and after any contamination event.
2. Send samples to the laboratory before the current report's validity ends (internal policy: at least 10 days before).
3. If any parameter fails: stop supply from the affected source, disinfect, re-test, and inform occupants.
