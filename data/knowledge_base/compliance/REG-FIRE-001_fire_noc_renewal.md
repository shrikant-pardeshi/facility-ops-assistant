# Fire NOC and Renewal Requirements

> **Illustrative paraphrase for demonstration only - not legal text.** Requirements, intervals and authorities vary by jurisdiction and are amended from time to time. Always verify against the current Act, Rules and the local authority before acting.

## What it is
A Fire No Objection Certificate (NOC) is issued by the Chief Fire Officer of the local fire authority confirming that the building's fire prevention and life safety measures meet the applicable requirements. Buildings receive a provisional NOC at plan approval and a final NOC before occupation. Many local authorities require periodic renewal of the NOC for high-rise and commercial buildings.

## Renewal - what the FM team must submit
- Application to the local fire authority in the prescribed format, with the renewal fee.
- Latest two half-yearly Form B certificates from a licensed fire agency (see REG-FIRE-002).
- Structural stability certificate (see REG-STR-001) and electrical installation safety certificate, where asked for by the authority.
- Fire drill records, fire system maintenance records and the list of trained fire marshals.
- Updated floor plans if any change of use or layout has occurred.

## Renewal lead time
Internal policy: file the renewal application at least 60 days before the NOC expiry date, so that the fire authority's site inspection can be completed before expiry.

## Consequences of lapse
Operating without a valid NOC may result in notices, penalties, or sealing/disconnection action by the authority, and may affect insurance cover. A lapse must be escalated to the Facility Manager and the building owner immediately.
