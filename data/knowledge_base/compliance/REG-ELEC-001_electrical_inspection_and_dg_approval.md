# Electrical Installation Periodic Inspection and DG Set Approval

> **Illustrative paraphrase for demonstration only - not legal text.** Requirements, intervals and authorities vary by jurisdiction and are amended from time to time. Always verify against the current Act, Rules and the local authority before acting.

## Periodic inspection of electrical installations
Installations above the specified voltage (e.g. HT substations, transformers) are subject to periodic inspection and testing by the Electrical Inspector (or self-certification where permitted) at the intervals prescribed in the regulations. The owner must keep the inspection certificate current and close all defects noted.

## DG set approval
Generating sets above the specified capacity require approval from the Electrical Inspector before being energised, and remain subject to periodic inspection. Changes to the DG installation (capacity, location, synchronising panel) need fresh approval.

## FM team responsibilities
1. Maintain test records: insulation resistance (IR), earth resistance, transformer oil tests (BDV/DGA) for oil-filled units, protection relay tests.
2. Apply for periodic inspection at least 60 days before the certificate expiry (internal policy).
3. Keep single-line diagrams and the licensed electrical contractor's test certificates ready for the inspection.

## Consequences of lapse
An expired inspection certificate is a statutory non-compliance and is commonly asked for during Fire NOC renewal and insurance audits.
