# Pollution Control Board Consent to Operate (STP and DG Sets)

> **Illustrative paraphrase for demonstration only - not legal text.** Requirements, intervals and authorities vary by jurisdiction and are amended from time to time. Always verify against the current Act, Rules and the local authority before acting.

## Requirement
Buildings operating a sewage treatment plant (STP) and DG sets typically require a Consent to Operate (CTO) from the State Pollution Control Board. The consent specifies limits for treated water quality (e.g. BOD, COD, TSS) and DG stack emissions, and is valid for a fixed period.

## FM team responsibilities
1. Monitor treated water quality monthly through an accredited laboratory and keep the reports.
2. Carry out DG stack emission monitoring at the frequency stated in the consent.
3. Submit periodic returns / environmental statements if required by the consent conditions.
4. Apply for renewal of the CTO well before expiry - internal policy is at least 60 days before the expiry date.

## Consequences of lapse
Operating without a valid consent may attract closure directions, penalties or disconnection of utilities. Escalate to the Facility Manager and the owner.
