# Form B - Half-yearly Fire System Compliance Certificate

> **Illustrative paraphrase for demonstration only - not legal text.** Requirements, intervals and authorities vary by jurisdiction and are amended from time to time. Always verify against the current Act, Rules and the local authority before acting.

## Requirement
The owner or occupier must ensure that the fire prevention and life safety measures are maintained in good repair and efficient condition, and must furnish a certificate in the prescribed form (Form B) from a licensed agency to the Chief Fire Officer twice a year - in January and in July.

## Scope of the licensed agency's inspection
- Fire pumps (main, diesel, jockey): performance test at rated head, auto-start verification.
- Hydrant and sprinkler network: pressure, landing valves, hose reels, sprinkler heads, alarm valves.
- Fire alarm and detection system: detectors, manual call points, panel, hooters, battery backup.
- Fire extinguishers, fire doors, escape routes, signage, emergency lighting, pressurisation fans.

## FM team responsibilities
1. Schedule the licensed agency inspection early in January and July.
2. Close all defects raised by the agency before the certificate is issued.
3. Submit the signed Form B to the fire authority and keep a copy on file.
4. The certificate for a cycle is treated as lapsed if the next cycle's certificate is not submitted by the end of January / July.

## Consequences of lapse
Non-submission is a statutory non-compliance and will be required at the time of Fire NOC renewal (see REG-FIRE-001).
