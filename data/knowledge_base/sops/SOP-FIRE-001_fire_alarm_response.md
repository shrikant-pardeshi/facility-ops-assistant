# Fire Alarm Activation Response

## First 3 minutes
1. The Fire Command Centre (FCC) operator notes the zone and device address shown on the fire alarm panel.
2. Send the nearest trained responder (fire marshal / security) to physically verify the location. Do NOT silence the alarm before verification.
3. If fire or smoke is confirmed: trigger evacuation for the affected floor and the floors above and below, call the fire brigade, and start the fire response plan.

## False or unwanted alarm
If verified as false (e.g. dust, steam, faulty detector): silence and reset the panel, record the cause, and raise a work order to clean or replace the detector. Three false alarms from the same detector in 30 days require replacement.

## Never do
- Never isolate a fire alarm zone without a work permit approved by the Fire & Safety Officer.
- Never leave the panel in "disabled" or "test" mode after maintenance.

## Records
Every activation - real or false - must be entered in the fire alarm log with time, zone, cause and action.
