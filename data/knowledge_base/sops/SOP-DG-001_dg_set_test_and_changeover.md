# DG Set Weekly No-load Test and Mains Changeover

## Weekly no-load test
1. Check fuel level (minimum 70% day tank; main tank as per stock policy), lube oil level, coolant level and battery voltage (above 24 V for a 24 V system) before start.
2. Start the DG in manual mode and run for 15 minutes without load.
3. Record voltage, frequency (50 Hz +/- 1%), oil pressure, coolant temperature and battery charging current.
4. Stop the DG and put it back in AUTO mode. Check the AMF panel shows "ready".

## Low battery voltage alarm
Check battery terminals for corrosion and tightness, check the battery charger output, and check battery specific gravity (for lead-acid). Slow cranking or repeated low-voltage alarms mean the batteries need replacement - raise a P2 work order.

## Monthly load test
Run the DG on building load for at least 30 minutes once a month, using a planned mains changeover. Inform occupants in advance.

## Records
Record every test in the DG log book. DG stack emission monitoring is required for pollution-control consent (see REG-ENV-001).
