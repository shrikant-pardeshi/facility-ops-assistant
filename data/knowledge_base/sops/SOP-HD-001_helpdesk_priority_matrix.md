# Helpdesk Ticket Priority Matrix and SLA

## Ticket types
- Work order: raised by facility staff, usually against a specific asset (breakdown, PPM follow-up, statutory action).
- Complaint: raised by an occupant about a service issue in their area (comfort, plumbing, housekeeping, access).

## Priority matrix
- P1 - Emergency (response 1 hour, restore 4 hours): fire or fire-system impairment, lift entrapment, total power failure, major water ingress, gas leak, security breach.
- P2 - Urgent (response 4 hours, restore 24 hours): breakdown of critical equipment (chiller, DG, UPS, fire pump, lift out of service), no cooling to a whole floor, STP failure.
- P3 - Normal (response 24 hours, restore 72 hours): localised comfort issues, minor leaks, lighting, access card faults, seepage, electrical faults in one area.
- P4 - Low (response 72 hours, restore 7 days): housekeeping, cosmetic repairs, general requests.

## Required information for every ticket
Requester ID, facility, exact location (floor and area), category, and a clear description of the problem. Asset ID is required for work orders on equipment where known.

## Duplicate tickets
Before creating a ticket, check for an existing ticket for the same issue (same facility, category and asset, or same floor). If an equivalent ticket is not yet closed, do not create a new one - update the requester with the existing ticket number and status.

## Escalation
If response SLA is breached: P1 to Facility Manager immediately; P2 to MEP Supervisor after SLA breach; P3/P4 in the daily review meeting.
