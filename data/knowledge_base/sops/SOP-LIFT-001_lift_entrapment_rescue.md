# Lift Entrapment Rescue

## Priority
A person trapped in a lift is always a P1 emergency. Response target: helpdesk acknowledges within 2 minutes; trained rescuer at the lift within 15 minutes.

## Immediate actions (helpdesk)
1. Talk to the trapped passengers on the intercom. Reassure them, ask how many people are inside and whether anyone is unwell.
2. Tell them NOT to try to open the doors or climb out.
3. Inform the lift AMC vendor's emergency number and the in-house trained lift rescuer.
4. If anyone is unwell, call the building medical/first-aid responder and emergency services.

## Rescue (trained personnel only)
1. Identify the lift and its position from the controller or display.
2. Switch OFF the main power supply to the lift at the machine room / controller and apply LOTO.
3. Follow the manufacturer's manual rescue procedure (brake release / rescue device) to move the car to the nearest landing.
4. Open the landing door with the emergency unlocking key and help passengers out.
5. Keep the lift out of service until the AMC vendor has inspected it.

## After the rescue
- Record time of alarm, arrival, and release. Raise a P1 work order for the vendor.
- Any accident involving a lift must be reported to the Inspector of Lifts as required by the applicable state lift legislation (see REG-LIFT-001).
