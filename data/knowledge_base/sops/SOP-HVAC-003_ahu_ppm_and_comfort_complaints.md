# AHU Preventive Maintenance and Comfort Complaint Handling

## Preventive maintenance schedule
- Monthly: clean pre-filters, check belt tension, check drain pan and condensate drain, check VFD and damper operation.
- Quarterly: clean fine filters, grease bearings, chemical coil cleaning, check supply air temperature (typical 13 to 15 C).
- Half-yearly: replace belts if worn, check fan alignment and vibration, calibrate return air temperature sensor.

## Handling "AC not cooling" or "too warm" complaints
1. Check the zone temperature and set-point on BMS (comfort band 23 +/- 1 C).
2. Check the AHU serving the zone is ON and the chilled water valve is modulating open.
3. Check the chilled water supply temperature at the AHU; if high, check the chiller plant.
4. Check filters for choking and the VAV/diffuser for the affected area.
5. If one room only: check for blocked diffusers, high occupancy, or a faulty VAV box.
Initial response is P3 unless the whole floor has no cooling (then P2).

## Handling "too cold" complaints
Check the set-point, VAV minimum position, and whether the diffuser is directly above the workstation. Adjust within the comfort band; do not change set-points for the whole floor for one complaint.

## Abnormal noise or vibration
Bearing or belt noise must be logged as a work order. If vibration is increasing, plan bearing replacement at the earliest shutdown window; running to failure can damage the shaft and fan wheel.

## VRF indoor unit water dripping
Usually a choked drain pan or condensate drain line, or a dirty filter causing coil icing. Switch off the indoor unit, place a tray, clean the drain line and filter, and restart.
