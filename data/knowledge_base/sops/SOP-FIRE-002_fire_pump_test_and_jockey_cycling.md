# Fire Pump Weekly Test and Jockey Pump Cycling

## Weekly auto-start test
1. Inform the FCC before starting the test.
2. Open the test drain valve to drop header pressure; confirm the jockey pump cuts in first, then the main electric pump starts automatically at its set cut-in pressure.
3. Run the electric pump for at least 10 minutes; record discharge pressure and motor current.
4. Test the diesel pump auto-start (by simulating electric pump failure) and run for 30 minutes weekly; check fuel, battery and cooling.
5. Return all pumps to AUTO mode. Pumps must never be left in MANUAL or OFF.

## Jockey pump cycling frequently
The jockey pump keeps the network pressurised. If it starts more often than about once an hour, suspect a leak in the hydrant/sprinkler network, a passing valve, or a faulty pressure switch.
1. Walk the risers and check hose reels, landing valves and sprinkler drain valves for leakage.
2. Check non-return valves and the pressure switch settings.
3. Raise a P2 work order. A fire system impairment must be reported to the Fire & Safety Officer.

## Records
The weekly test log is required evidence for the half-yearly Form B certificate (see REG-FIRE-002).
